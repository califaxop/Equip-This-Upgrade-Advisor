# Equip This!: Upgrade Advisor

**Build your character the way you want to play it!**

Gear advice worked out from how *you* play, for the character *you* want to build.

Equip This! isn't a list of stat weights. It tries every item on your own character (your stats, your talents, your abilities, and the way you actually fight) and tells you what it does for the character you're building: a hard hitter, a healer who never runs dry, a tank, someone who survives every pull, or a mix. Then it marks the upgrades on tooltips and glows the best quest reward.

## Build the character you want
- **You decide what "better" means.** One slider, from More Damage (or More Healing; More Threat for tanks) to More Survivability, with presets: Damage, Leveling (the default while you level), Balanced, Survival / Tanking. Every tooltip says what its verdict is for: "Upgrade +4.2% for your build (75% damage / 25% survival)".
- **Tank, heal or fight players.** Tick "I tank" with any class for threat and a tank's survivability (your stance, form or aura's threat, from Forever's own data). Healers get healing and mana. Optional PvP numbers (burst, sustained damage and survival against players) use PvP's own assumptions, not PvE's.
- **Gear for where you're going.** Pick a spec other than the one your talents say, and the raid you're gearing for at 60 (how much of what hits you is magic). Set how long your fights last, and Intellect, Spirit and mana regen are worth what they're worth over that fight.

## Numbers from how you play
- **It learns your rotation.** After each fight, Equip This! reads the game's damage meter and learns how often you use each ability and spell. Weave shocks between Stormstrikes, and spell power counts for you; don't, and it won't. A new character starts from a default (real raid logs) and becomes yours after about 8 fights. It all stays on your computer.
- **Your character, not a template.** Your stats come straight from your character sheet; your talents, weapon skill, weapon imbue (Windfury...), procs, set bonuses and enchants all count, with Forever's own spell coefficients, cooldowns, proc rates and threat values from the game's data.
- **Every number apart, and honest.** Each tooltip says what the item is tried with ("vs [your item]" or "paired with [the shield in your bags]"), then damage (or healing, or threat) and survivability (effective health) separately, then the verdict for your build. Hold Shift for where the numbers come from and how sure they are. Effects it can't count are named, never quietly treated as nothing.

## Gear advice where you look
- **Every tooltip:** upgrade or downgrade, by how much, and why.
- **Smart comparisons:** both ring and trinket slots, two-handers against main hand + off hand, a one-hander paired with your best off-hand or shield from your bags, set bonuses gained and lost, your enchant carried over, unique items, and "your Axes skill is 180: until it's trained up" for a weapon type you haven't trained.
- **Quest rewards:** the best choice for your build glows. No upgrade among them? The one that sells for most gets a coin.
- **All classes and specs:** melee, hunters (ranged), casters, healers and tanks.

## Help make it better (optional)
Equip This! gets better with real fights from every class. Tick "Help improve Equip This!" in `/eqt` to keep anonymous fight records: your class, spec, level, each fight's length, your damage and healing per spell, and Equip This!'s own estimate. Never your name, realm, guild, other players or IDs, and nothing leaves the game on its own. "Submit fight logs" (or `/eqt report`) gives you the form's link and your logs to paste into it, and shows exactly what's in them; at most twice a week (after Wednesday's maintenance and on Sunday), with 25 or more new fights waiting, a line in chat reminds you. "I reported this" clears them, so the next export holds only new fights; records you don't send are cleared when you log in again, so they never pile up.

## Quick start
1. Install and play: Equip This! works out of the box.
2. Type `/eqt` to pick your spec and slide toward the character you want to build.
3. Play: after about 8 fights, the numbers follow how you play.

## Commands
| Command | Does |
|---|---|
| `/eqt` (or `/equipthis`) | Open settings |
| `/eqt item <item>` | The full working for an item (shift-click it into the command) |
| `/eqt model` | Your character as Equip This! sees it: damage parts, rotation, threat, PvP, effective health |
| `/eqt report` | Submit your fight logs (with "Help improve Equip This!" ticked) |
| `/eqt bug` | Bugs & feedback: the form's link and a line to paste into it |
| `/eqn` | The engine's diagnostics (`/eqn measure`, `/eqn abilities`, `/eqn weights`...) |

## Good to know
- Equip-line stats and effects are read from English item tooltips.
- A brand-new character starts from a default rotation; it becomes yours after about 8 fights.

## Credits
Forever's own game tables (spells, items, enchants, proc rates, threat), via [wago.tools](https://wago.tools). Default rotations, raid damage mixes and server-kept proc rates: [Warcraft Logs](https://www.warcraftlogs.com), from WoW Classic (Era) 40-player raids, not Forever's own: Forever's versions may differ, and your own fights replace the rotations.

Tooltips show the verdict and, under it, DPS (or healing, or threat for tanks) and effective health apart; hold Shift for the working and how sure it is. At the level cap your EHP counts that raid's spell damage, measured on Warcraft Logs (6 recent kills of each boss), so the right resistances count: fire in Molten Core (tanks take 32% of their damage as magic, everyone else 76%) and Blackwing Lair (32% / 85%), nature in Ahn'Qiraj (17% / 63%), a mix in Naxxramas (12% / 69%). `/eqt guide` says which raid it's using.
- **4.2.0:** Resistances count: your EHP now includes spell damage, cut by your resistances the Classic way (up to 75%), so resistance gear raises survivability. While leveling, 20% of what comes at you is magic, split evenly. At the level cap it follows Naxxramas, measured on Warcraft Logs (90 kills from 40-player raids): tanks take 12% of it as magic (shadow, fire, nature, frost), everyone else 75% (nature, shadow, frost), so at 60 a damage dealer's or healer's survivability is mostly Stamina and resistances, not armor or dodge. New "I tank" option: any class can be scored as a tank (bosses hit you; survival first). `/eqt guide` shows how much of an enemy's melee and spells you take. Stat weights, talent builds, rotations and calibration refreshed from Forever Upgrades; proc rates refreshed from the game's tables.
- **4.1.0:** Survivability is now your effective health (EHP), worked out with Forever Upgrades' own model for tanks: health over the share of melee damage you take after armor, misses, dodges, parries, blocks, crits and crushing blows (checked against its sim). Dodge, parry, Defense and block now count for everyone's survivability, and tanks are scored by their EHP (plus damage from their class's damage spec, now calibrated too) instead of by weights. Effective health is called EHP throughout.
- **4.0.0:** Rebuilt around Forever Upgrades' numbers. Items score their stats times the site's weights for your spec (plus effects), as a % of your whole output (your gear plus what your character brings, calibrated with Forever Upgrades' Sim DPS, so the % is close to the change in your DPS / HPS), with your talents scaling the stats they feed, hit / expertise / defense caps against your gear, and a build that mixes damage and survival (Damage, Leveling, Balanced, Survival or your own mix). The slider (More Damage or Healing to More Survivability) is the only thing to set. Hold Shift on an item for where its score comes from. The DPS simulator and the Your DPS window are gone (and with them buffs, imbues and live stats: the same gear always scores the same).
